# Third-party notices

## Avalonia ColorPicker theme templates

`Common/Frontend/Assets/Styles/ColorPickerThemes.axaml` contains app-owned adaptations of the Avalonia.Controls.ColorPicker 12.1.4 Fluent `ColorView.xaml`, `ColorSpectrum.xaml`, `ColorPreviewer.xaml`, and `ColorSlider.xaml` templates, together with local ColorView, ColorSpectrum, ColorPreviewer, and ColorSlider themes/resources. The ColorView numeric-editor theme is app-authored and retains the framework template-part contract.
